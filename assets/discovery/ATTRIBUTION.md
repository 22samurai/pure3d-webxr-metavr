# Space Shuttle Discovery sample models

These two decoded GLB files are preview and low-detail derivatives from the Smithsonian Institution's **Space Shuttle Discovery Voyager Scene** ([Zenodo record 18713310](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18713310)), published under **CC BY 4.0**. They were converted from the record's Draco-compressed GLBs with `tools/Convert-DracoGlbForGodot.ps1` and glTF Transform 4.5.1. The original source files were not changed.

The decoded models are supplied as an import and performance test for Godot. The remaining four detail levels and the conversion manifest are in the companion output package, `Space_Shuttle_Discovery_Godot_GLBS.zip`. Voyager articles, images, and annotations are separate source files and are not imported with these models.
